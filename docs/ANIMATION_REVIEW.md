## Columns

- **Clip / Rig / Kind / Duration**: the clip id, its rig, attack / loop / one-shot, seconds and frames at 60 fps.
- **Anticipation / Snap / Smear / Hold / Settle / Recovery**: for an attack, `Y <frames>` when the beat is defined, `N` when it is missing. Section 10 budgets:
  Anticipation 4..10 frames, Snap 2..4, Smear 2, Settle 6..12 (enemies may hold Anticipation longer: the wind-up).
- **Hit-stop**: the clip's tier (Normal 3 / Final 6 / Kill 10 frames, section 6) or `-`.
- **Hits**: HitWindowOpen / HitWindowClose pairs.
- **Trail vs windows**: how many frames before the first hit window the trail ignites and how many after the last it goes
  off (allowed: 6 early, 8 late); `uncovered` names a window with no trail, `stray` a trail span outside every window.
- **Root motion**: studs between the first and last root key.
- **Problems found**: everything the review rules (`src/client/dev/ReviewMarkers.luau`) flag; `none` is the goal.
- **Watched at 0.25x**: hand-filled; `yes <date>` once the clip was watched frame by frame in the ClipLab.
- **Problems seen**: hand-filled; what looked wrong against section 2 / section 10 while watching.
- **Fixed**: hand-filled; the clip edit or commit that fixed it, or `open`.
